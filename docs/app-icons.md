# App icon sources

Brand marks on the homepage Many MCPs card are stored locally so the page does
not depend on a third-party icon request at render time.

* `lunch-money.ico` - Lunch Money emblem from
  <https://lunchmoney.app/favicon.ico>. Usage follows the
  [Lunch Money media kit](https://lunchmoney.app/media-kit/).
* `reddit.svg` - Reddit mark from <https://simpleicons.org/>.
* `steam.svg` - Steam mark from <https://simpleicons.org/>.

The marks identify their respective services and do not imply endorsement.
